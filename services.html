Core Offerings

Specialized Expertise

Coverage Advisory

Thorough policy review and coverage guidance to ensure you are neither overpaying nor under-protected. We analyze modifiers and class codes.

Claims Support

Assistance throughout the entire claims lifecycle. We act as your liaison with adjusters to ensure prompt, fair resolution and mitigate long-term impacts.

Risk Assessment

Identifying physical and operational workplace exposure areas before they result in claims, through detailed on-site and data-driven analysis.

Safety Program Guidance

Supporting the creation, implementation, and maintenance of workplace safety improvement strategies designed specifically for your industry.

Compliance Advisory

Organizing documentation, certificates, and compliance requirements to ensure you meet all state and federal regulations seamlessly.

Premium Review

Analyzing premium-related factors, experience modification rates (X-Mod), and historical loss data to uncover potential savings.

The Advisory Journey

Discover

We start by understanding your business, gathering current policies, loss runs, and operational data to form a baseline.

Review

Our analysts thoroughly review the data to identify gaps, overcharges, and areas of significant risk exposure.

Advise

We present actionable recommendations to restructure coverage, optimize premiums, and enhance safety protocols.

Implement

We work directly with you to put these strategies into action, from policy adjustments to safety training.

Monitor

Continuous oversight of claims, compliance, and renewals to ensure the strategy remains effective long-term.
