Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common inquiries about our advisory services.

What types of businesses do you work with?

We work with a diverse range of industries, including construction, manufacturing, healthcare, and professional services, tailoring our advisory to the specific risk profile of your sector.

How long does a coverage review typically take?

A standard coverage and premium review takes about 2 to 3 weeks, depending on the complexity of your current policies and how quickly we can access your historical data.

Do you replace our current insurance broker?

No, we act as an independent advisory firm. We work alongside your existing broker to provide objective analysis and ensure your coverage is optimized without conflicts of interest.

What happens if we have an active claim?

We can step in immediately to review the claim, facilitate communication with the adjuster, and provide strategic guidance to help resolve the claim efficiently while mitigating further risk.

How is your advisory service priced?

Our pricing is transparent and typically based on a retainer or project fee depending on the scope of work. We discuss all costs upfront during our initial consultation.
