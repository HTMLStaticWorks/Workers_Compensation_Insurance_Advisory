Sector Statutory Matrix

Statutory Coverage Structure & Directives Matrix

Statutory requirements, policy endorsements, and workforce protection standards vary fundamentally across commercial sectors. Review the statutory policy structures, mandatory endorsements, and OSHA safety compliance directives tailored to your industry below.

Construction & Commercial Contracting

Statutory Codes: 5403, 5445, 5606, 5022
Statutory Policy Structure & Limits
  • Part One statutory medical and wage indemnity coverage structured for multi-jurisdiction jobsite reciprocity.
  • Part Two Employers' Liability elevated to $1M/$1M/$1M minimum to satisfy general contractor master service agreements.
  • Item 3.C Other States Coverage actively declared for cross-border tradesmen, staging yards, and regional deployments.
Mandatory Endorsements & Audit Rules
  • Blanket Waiver of Subrogation (WC 00 03 13) endorsements systematically bound for project principals.
  • Rigorous subcontractor Certificate of Insurance (COI) auditing to eliminate costly uninsured contractor payroll penalties.
  • Overtime and premium pay segregation compliance to prevent inflated annual payroll audit assessments.
Statutory Workforce Protection (OSHA)
  • OSHA 29 CFR 1926 Subpart M 100% tie-off fall arrest protocols for elevated decking, scaffolds, and roofing.
  • Competent Person trenching and excavation shoring inspections under 29 CFR 1926 Subpart P standards.
  • Silica and respiratory protection compliance adhering to statutory 29 CFR 1926.1153 permissible exposure limits.

Precision Manufacturing & Industrial Fabrication

Statutory Codes: 3632, 3179, 3076, 3507
Statutory Policy Structure & Limits
  • Full occupational disease and repetitive motion cumulative trauma statutory exposure indemnity.
  • Employers' Liability coverage engineered to defend against third-party machine manufacturer action-over lawsuits.
  • Stop-Gap Employers' Liability endorsements attached for monopolistic fund states (OH, WA, WY, ND).
Mandatory Endorsements & Audit Rules
  • Alternate Employer Endorsement (WC 00 03 01) covering temporary staffing agency machinists and line operators.
  • Strict shop floor vs clerical (Code 8810) payroll segregation to prevent class code misallocation during audit.
  • Voluntary Compensation Endorsement (WC 00 03 11) for specialized engineers and technical supervisory staff.
Statutory Workforce Protection (OSHA)
  • OSHA 29 CFR 1910.147 Control of Hazardous Energy (Lockout/Tagout) zero-energy state verification procedures.
  • Point-of-operation machine safeguarding conforming to 29 CFR 1910.212 optical light curtains and interlocks.
  • Occupational noise exposure monitoring and statutory audiometric baseline testing under 29 CFR 1910.95.

Healthcare Systems, Senior Living & Clinics

Statutory Codes: 8832, 8829, 9040, 8835
Statutory Policy Structure & Limits
  • Statutory disease presumption coverage for communicable airborne pathogens and healthcare-acquired infections.
  • Employers' Liability tailored for needle-stick and biomedical exposure liability without restrictive sub-limits.
  • Volunteer and student intern workers' compensation endorsements to eliminate disputed non-payroll injury claims.
Mandatory Endorsements & Audit Rules
  • Multi-facility location schedule endorsements ensuring seamless coverage across regional medical campuses.
  • Traveling healthcare worker statutory endorsements extending coverage to mobile phlebotomy and home visits.
  • Agency and per-diem nursing payroll audit classification defense to prevent carrier duplicate billing.
Statutory Workforce Protection (OSHA)
  • OSHA 29 CFR 1910.1030 Bloodborne Pathogens Standard statutory sharps safety containment systems.
  • Safe patient biomechanical handling protocols utilizing mechanical ceiling lifts to prevent lumbar strain.
  • Workplace violence prevention programs conforming to OSHA Section 5(a)(1) General Duty Clause requirements.

Warehousing, Fleet Logistics & Supply Chain

Statutory Codes: 7219, 8292, 7380, 8291
Statutory Policy Structure & Limits
  • Part One multi-state statutory coverage covering interstate logistics and regional cross-dock corridors.
  • Inter-state reciprocity agreements ensuring rapid claim jurisdiction resolution during cross-country freight haulage.
  • Excess Workers' Compensation structures structured over self-insured retention (SIR) for enterprise fleets.
Mandatory Endorsements & Audit Rules
  • Independent contractor / owner-operator 1099 non-employee declaration filing and statutory verification.
  • Driver per-diem vs gross taxable wage payroll audit reconciliation under standard rating bureau regulations.
  • WC 00 03 05 Joint Venture Endorsement protecting 3PL shared distribution operations.
Statutory Workforce Protection (OSHA)
  • OSHA 29 CFR 1910.178 Powered Industrial Trucks 3-year statutory recertification and daily inspection tracking.
  • Loading dock edge fall barriers and automatic trailer restraint locks to eliminate drive-away crush incidents.
  • Ergonomic high-speed picking protocols adhering to NIOSH lifting equation limits and pallet turntable setups.

Retail Chains, E-Commerce & Wholesale

Statutory Codes: 8017, 8018, 8033, 8006
Statutory Policy Structure & Limits
  • Part One statutory slip-and-fall indemnity and customer assistance physical injury coverage.
  • Employers' Liability protecting against supervisor-directed heavy merchandise handling claims.
  • Territory coverage endorsements extending to pop-up storefronts and seasonal holiday fulfillment hubs.
Mandatory Endorsements & Audit Rules
  • Seasonal workforce payroll surge reporting protocols to avoid surprise year-end premium audit spikes.
  • Accurate payroll segregation between clerical managers (8810) and active floor inventory handlers (8017).
  • Outside sales and field merchandiser (8742) travel expense reimbursement audit segregation.
Statutory Workforce Protection (OSHA)
  • Walkway audit compliance (ANSI/NFSI B101 slip resistance) and high-traffic entryway moisture mitigation.
  • OSHA 1910.176 Material Handling standards preventing stockroom carton cascade and shelving collapse.
  • Rolling step ladder safety protocols (OSHA 1910.23) prohibiting unstable stool misuse during merchandising.

Corporate Offices, Tech Firms & Professional Services

Statutory Codes: 8810, 8742, 8601, 8803
Statutory Policy Structure & Limits
  • Part One statutory coverage for remote and hybrid home-office musculoskeletal injuries occurring within course of employment.
  • Foreign Voluntary Workers' Compensation (FVWC) covering international travel, executive summits, and overseas assignments.
  • Employers' Liability coverage for remote work repetitive strain disputes and commercial landlord hazard subrogation.
Mandatory Endorsements & Audit Rules
  • Telecommuter state endorsements verifying statutory compliance in all states where distributed staff reside.
  • Corporate officer exclusion/inclusion endorsement (WC 00 03 08) aligned to executive ownership and equity stakes.
  • Executive compensation payroll caps applied per state rating bureau thresholds to prevent overpaid audit premiums.
Statutory Workforce Protection (OSHA)
  • Ergonomic workstation digital self-assessment certifications for home-office and on-premise desk setups.
  • Emergency Action Plan (OSHA 29 CFR 1910.38) and certified floor warden evacuation procedures for high-rise offices.
  • Indoor Air Quality (IAQ) and ASHRAE ventilation compliance audits for high-density office environments.
