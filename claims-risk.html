The Resolution Process

01
Notification

Timely reporting is critical. We assist in establishing protocols for immediate incident reporting to ensure all statutory deadlines are met and evidence is preserved accurately.

02
Documentation

We guide your team in compiling necessary medical records, witness accounts, and internal safety reports to build a comprehensive claim file.

03
Review & Triage

Every claim is evaluated for validity and severity. We coordinate with adjusters to ensure claims are categorized correctly and assigned appropriate reserves.

04
Communication

Maintaining clear, consistent communication between the employer, the injured employee, medical providers, and the carrier to avoid litigation and confusion.

05
Resolution & Return

Facilitating swift claim closure and structuring effective return-to-work programs that accommodate restrictions while maintaining productivity.

06
Follow-up Analysis

Post-claim analysis to identify root causes and implement targeted safety interventions to prevent recurrence.
